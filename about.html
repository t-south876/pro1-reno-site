About PRO-1 RENO

Building excellence in home renovation across Woodstock and Oxford County

Your Trusted Renovation Partner

Pro-1 Reno is a premier residential renovation and general contracting company based in Woodstock, Ontario, serving discerning homeowners throughout Oxford County and surrounding areas. We specialize in delivering exceptional craftsmanship, refined finishes, and expertly managed renovations that elevate both the beauty and functionality of your home.

From custom bathrooms and kitchens to full basement conversions and structural renovations, Pro-1 Reno approaches every project with precision, transparency, and a commitment to excellence. We collaborate closely with our clients, providing thoughtful planning, clear communication, and meticulous execution, ensuring every detail meets the highest standards and complies fully with Ontario building codes.

At Pro-1 Reno, quality is never compromised. Our mission is to create timeless spaces that add lasting value while offering a seamless, professional renovation experience from concept to completion.
